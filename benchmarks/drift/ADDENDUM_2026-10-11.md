# Addendum 2026-10-11 (IST) to the dependency-drift protocol

`SELECTION_PROTOCOL.md` is unchanged. After its method hashes were first recorded
(`FREEZE_MANIFEST.v0.json`), and **before any candidate was logged or screened**, the
method changed once:

- `engine.py`, `models.py` and `api.py`: noisy incidents judged by per-comparison
  Student-t intervals are now capped at `inferred` unless their runs never varied. The
  pre-registered noise-guarantee simulation, version 2, found wrong `verified` results
  with those intervals; see `benchmarks/results/noise_guarantee_v2/REPORT.md`.
  `verified` for noisy metrics now needs `interval: anytime` with a declared `noise_scale`.

The only search activity before this change was three GitHub issue-search queries, whose
result titles were seen; none was opened, screened or logged. They are listed first in
`SEARCH_LOG.md`. The change concerns noisy replays only. Drift incidents use one
deterministic run per replay, so their decisions do not depend on it.
`FREEZE_MANIFEST.json` was regenerated after the change and is the one evaluation checks.
